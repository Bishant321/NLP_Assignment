## Section C — Open-ended Questions

**Q1: What did you like most about the chatbot?**  
> I was genuinely impressed that it has no internet requirement and no paid API. The knowledge base is comprehensive — I asked about Langtang Valley which many travel chatbots don't know well, and it gave a solid answer. The 7-day and 14-day itinerary suggestions were exactly what I needed for planning.

**Q2: What was the most confusing or frustrating thing?**  
> Loading time for the full RAG model was slow on the first run. Also, I tried asking "Is it safe to travel alone as a woman in Nepal?" and the bot didn't have a specific response for that — it fell back to a generic answer. Women's solo travel safety is a common concern that should be addressed.

**Q3: Did the chatbot feel like it "understood" you?**  
> Mostly yes. Standard tourism questions were handled well. Where it struggled was with comparative or nuanced questions — "which trek is best for someone with only 5 days?" required me to phrase it more specifically.

**Q4: Would you use this chatbot to plan a real Nepal trip?**  
> Definitely as a planning tool, yes. I'm actually planning a Nepal trip next year and I used it to draft a rough itinerary. It saved me a lot of initial research time. I'd recommend it to friends.

**Q5: Any suggestions for improvement?**  
> - Add women's solo travel safety information  
> - Include a "What's your budget?" initial question to personalise recommendations  
> - Show weather information by month in a table format  
> - A "translate to Nepali" feature for common phrases would be great for travellers  
> - The startup message could be shorter
