## Section C — Open-ended Questions

**Q1: What did you like most about the chatbot?**  
> The quick question buttons on the bottom are really handy. I liked how the chatbot knew so much detail about different regions — Pokhara, Chitwan, Everest. The interface looks clean and professional with the mountain header.

**Q2: What was the most confusing or frustrating thing?**  
> When I asked a very specific question like "how do I get from Namche Bazaar to Dingboche", the chatbot gave a general trekking answer instead. It needs better handling of route-specific questions.

**Q3: Did the chatbot feel like it "understood" you?**  
> Yes, mostly. When I asked about "best food to try", it gave exactly the kind of answer I was looking for — Dal Bhat, Momo, Sel Roti. It felt more natural than just reading a guidebook.

**Q4: Would you use this chatbot to plan a real Nepal trip?**  
> I would use it as a starting point, yes. It's great for getting an overview quickly. I would still cross-check visa costs and trek permits with official sources.

**Q5: Any suggestions for improvement?**  
> - Add a feature to remember my previous questions in the session  
> - Include approximate costs for each trek route  
> - Add photos or links to images of the destinations  
> - A "compare destinations" feature would be useful (e.g. "Everest vs Annapurna for beginners")
